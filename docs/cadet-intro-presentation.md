# Flight Points — Cadet Introduction Presentation
*Slide-by-slide outline for briefing cadets on the Flight Points system.*

---

## Slide 1 — Title
**Flight Points**
*Recognising Achievement. Rewarding Excellence.*

- Squadron / Flight branding banner
- Date / Academic Year
- Presented by: [SNCO Name / Flight Point Lead]
- URL: **flightpoints.uk**

---

## Slide 2 — What is Flight Points?
**A digital system for tracking cadet achievement across the squadron.**

Key points:
- Records and celebrates cadet contributions — not just parades
- Covers: attendance, attitude, extra effort, special duties, and more
- Visible in real-time to cadets via the website
- Replaces manual tally sheets and spreadsheets

> "Every effort counts — Flight Points makes sure it's noticed."

---

## Slide 3 — Our Aim
**Why does this system exist?**

- **Recognise** cadets who go above and beyond
- **Motivate** consistent effort and participation
- **Reward** outstanding performance through prizes and recognition
- **Be fair** — transparent, consistent, and visible to everyone
- **Support staff** with a clear record of cadet activity and engagement

---

## Slide 4 — How is the Squadron Structured in Flight Points?
**Flights and Roles**

| Group | Who |
|-------|-----|
| **Flights** | Individual cadet flights (e.g. A, B, C Flight) |
| **HQ** | Staff, Officers, and Warrant Officers |
| **NCO** | Senior cadets — cannot receive points (they give them) |

Points are awarded **within flights** by designated Point Givers (NCOs, Staff) and confirmed by SNCOs.

---

## Slide 5 — How Do Points Work?
**Earning Points**

Points can be awarded for (examples):
- Attending a parade night
- Completing a training module
- Showing exceptional effort or attitude
- Representing the squadron at events
- Helping with squadron activities

**Important rules:**
- HQ cadets and NCOs do **not** receive points — they award them
- Points are given by staff and NCOs — not self-awarded
- Each award is logged with who gave it, when, and why

---

## Slide 6 — Viewing Your Points
**Access your profile at flightpoints.uk**

What you can see when logged in:
- Your **total points** and **rank** within your flight
- A **leaderboard** showing standings across the squadron
- Each individual **point award** — who gave it and the reason
- Your **attendance record**
- Available and upcoming **rewards**

Your account is created for you — your SNCO will hand you your login credentials.

---

## Slide 7 — Rewards
**Points can unlock real rewards.**

How it works:
- Active rewards are listed on the Rewards tab
- Points thresholds or nomination-based selection
- Top cadets may be selected for prizes, outings, or special recognition
- **Suggestions welcome** — any cadet can suggest a new reward idea
- Rewards are voted on and approved by staff before going live

Reward stages: **Active → Claimed (winner announced) → Expired**

---

## Slide 8 — Attendance
**Your attendance is tracked alongside your points.**

- Every parade night is logged
- Bulk attendance is saved by your flight NCO or staff
- You can see your personal attendance history on the website
- Attendance contributes toward overall recognition — not just points

If you believe your attendance is marked incorrectly, raise a **Ticket** (see next slide).

---

## Slide 9 — Raising a Ticket
**Got a question, dispute, or request? Use the Ticket system.**

- Found in the **Tickets tab** when logged in
- Use it for:
  - Querying a points award
  - Reporting a missing attendance mark
  - Any other enquiry about your record
- Tickets are reviewed by staff — you will receive a response in the system

*Keep queries respectful and factual.*

---

## Slide 10 — Your Account
**Getting Started**

1. Your SNCO will create your account — no self-registration required
2. You'll receive a **username** and a **password** (e.g. `EagleBolt47` format)
3. Visit **flightpoints.uk** and log in
4. You can only see your own data and flight leaderboard
5. Keep your password safe — contact your SNCO if you need a reset

**Your login:** Username only (no email address needed)

---

## Slide 11 — Roles at a Glance
**Who can do what?**

| Role | What They Can Do |
|------|-----------------|
| **Cadet** | View own points, leaderboard, rewards, attendance, raise tickets |
| **Point Giver** (NCO) | Award points to their own flight, mark attendance |
| **Staff** | Award points across flights, manage attendance |
| **SNCO** | Full admin — manage accounts, rewards, cadets, all data |
| **Presentation** | Display-only mode for squadron screens / parade nights |

---

## Slide 12 — Key Rules to Remember
**The Flight Points Code**

1. Points are earned — not given out of favouritism
2. The system is transparent — everyone can see the leaderboard
3. Disputes go through the Ticket system, not informal chat
4. Accounts are personal — do not share your login
5. NCOs and Staff are trusted to award points fairly and accurately
6. End-of-term or end-of-year reviews may use Flight Points in assessments

---

## Slide 13 — Questions?
**Any questions?**

- Website: **flightpoints.uk**
- Problems logging in? See your SNCO
- Questions about a points award? Raise a Ticket on the website

> *Flight Points is here to make recognition fair, visible, and motivating for every cadet.*

---

## Appendix A — Slide Design Notes
- **Colour scheme:** Use squadron / ATC branding colours (blue/grey/gold recommended)
- **Font:** Clean sans-serif (e.g. Calibri, Open Sans, or Poppins)
- **Images:** Include cadet activity photos where available (with permissions)
- **Tone:** Positive, motivating, and straightforward — avoid jargon
- **Length:** Aim for 10–13 slides; do not overload each slide with text
- **Animations:** Minimal — fade-in bullet points are fine; avoid distracting transitions
